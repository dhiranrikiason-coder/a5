Our Founding Purpose

Why Traditional Math Instruction Fails Young Minds

For decades, mathematics education has suffered from an artificial divide: on one side, mindless computational drills that induce acute anxiety; on the other, disconnected theoretical abstractions that leave learners feeling bewildered and incapable.

NumeracyBoost was founded on the bedrock conviction that mathematical ability is a universally accessible cognitive capacity. By grounding abstract symbolic operations in spatial, visual, and tactile mental models, any motivated student can develop genuine mathematical creativity.

Today, our research-backed curriculum empowers thousands of scholars across public school systems, selective STEM academies, and international competitive mathematics circles.

Cognitive Psychophysics

We leverage working-memory management, spaced retrieval practice, and cognitive load theory to ensure mathematical concepts are consolidated directly into long-term procedural memory.

Singapore Bar Modeling

Visual representation bridges the chasm between arithmetic word problems and algebraic equations, giving students a dependable cognitive scaffolding for non-routine problem solving.

Olympiad Rigor

We train students in the non-routine heuristic problem decomposition methods used by international mathematical olympiad competitors, fostering resilient, flexible minds.

The Boston Research Center

Empirical Research & Teacher Training

Headquartered in Boston, Massachusetts, the NumeracyBoost Institute conducts ongoing empirical classroom studies and delivers certified professional development masterclasses to mathematics faculty worldwide.

Our research fellows continuously publish peer-reviewed monographs on mathematical cognition, subitizing kinematics, and anxiety remediation, ensuring our software and lesson modules incorporate current scientific discoveries.
