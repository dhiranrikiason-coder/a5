Transparent Cookie Governance

Cookie Policy & Digital Tracking Protocols

Clear documentation detailing our use of HTTP cookies, local storage tokens, analytical scripts, and student privacy protections across all NumeracyBoost digital properties.

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Last Revised: February 2026 • Regulatory Review Status: Fully Certified

1. Understanding Cookies and Transparent Digital Governance

NumeracyBoost is dedicated to maintaining complete transparency regarding the digital tracking technologies, session identifiers, and local data storage mechanisms deployed across our web properties. This Cookie Policy explains in clear, comprehensive language what cookies are, how we utilize them to support your educational experience, and what granular choices you have regarding their activation. HTTP cookies are compact text files transmitted by web servers to your client browser, where they are stored locally to remember preferences, authenticate sessions, and track technical telemetry. In authenticated student learning spaces, we prioritize absolute data minimization, ensuring that digital cookies serve strictly functional and diagnostic instructional purposes rather than behavioral advertising agendas. By continuing to navigate our educational domain, you acknowledge our principled utilization of cookies in strict alignment with this policy. We empower learners and parents with direct control over optional analytical tracking mechanisms at all times.

Our cookie governance structure is designed to strictly adhere to leading international digital privacy frameworks, including the EU ePrivacy Directive, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and applicable global student data statutes. Under these stringent regulatory regimes, non-essential cookies and persistent analytical scripts cannot be deployed on user computing hardware without prior, freely given, informed consent. We do not engage in covert digital fingerprinting, canvas tracking, cross-site identity resolution, or invasive behavioral profiling across unassociated internet domains. When minor students access our curriculum modules, our systems automatically suppress all non-essential analytics, ensuring that young learners interact exclusively with secure, essential functional cookies. School district technology directors can confidently whitelist our web domain, knowing our tracking footprint is tightly compartmentalized and academically focused. We continually audit our digital perimeter to ensure that third-party scripts never compromise pupil confidentiality.

2. Classification of Cookies Deployed Across NumeracyBoost

Strictly Essential Cookies constitute the foundational technical bedrock of our platform and cannot be deactivated without fundamentally breaking website navigation and account security. These essential cookies manage user session authentication, prevent cross-site request forgery attacks, balance computational loads across server clusters, and maintain secure access to student skill portfolios. Without these cookies, authenticated services such as diagnostic assessment generation, interactive problem-solving environments, and real-time student-teacher communications could not function reliably or securely. Because these cookies are strictly necessary for service delivery, they do not require prior consent under applicable privacy legislation, though we remain fully transparent regarding their operational purpose. Essential cookies typically expire automatically upon browser termination or after thirty days of authenticated inactivity, leaving no persistent tracking residue on user hardware. They never capture personal demographic information or unencrypted student academic records.

Performance and Analytical Cookies enable our instructional engineering team to evaluate how users interact with our mathematical modules, identifying navigational bottlenecks and rendering latencies. These cookies collect aggregated, anonymous computational metrics such as page visit frequencies, average problem session durations, module abandonment points, and client browser rendering speeds. By analyzing this aggregated telemetry, our cognitive psychologists and software architects determine which geometric visualizers or arithmetic drills require UI simplification or computational acceleration. Analytical data streams are processed utilizing rigorous IP anonymization techniques and aggregated into statistical cohorts that cannot be correlated back to identifiable individual students or specific classroom cohorts. We utilize enterprise analytics solutions configured to prevent analytical vendors from co-mingling our educational telemetry with their proprietary advertising profiles. You retain the absolute right to accept or decline these analytical cookies through our centralized preference center.

3. Functional Cookies and Customization Preferences

Functional Cookies allow our educational software to remember the custom interface selections, visual accessibility preferences, and pedagogical configurations established by individual learners and educators. For example, these cookies record your preferred display color contrast theme, font sizing adjustments, mathematical notation format conventions (such as fractional display styles), and audio narration volume levels. By persisting these preferences locally across consecutive learning sessions, functional cookies eliminate the frustrating necessity of reconfiguring accessibility settings every time a student opens a new lesson module. Functional cookies also facilitate interactive features such as retaining scratchpad work during momentary network disconnections and remembering your last completed lesson in a multi-part learning sequence. If you choose to disable functional cookies through your browser settings, the core learning modules will remain accessible, but personalized interface preferences will revert to default settings upon every page reload. These cookies remain active for up to twelve months to support consistent multi-term academic continuity.

In addition to visual display preferences, functional cookies support localized instructional pacing, allowing the software to deliver diagnostic exercises in alignment with the student's designated grade tier and regional curriculum framework. These cookies do not track student browsing habits across external commercial websites or transmit personal behavioral markers to data broker databases. All preference data stored within functional cookies is encrypted and restricted to the NumeracyBoost domain through strict SameSite and Secure cookie attributes. When shared computing hardware is utilized in school computer laboratories, educators can execute single-click cache and cookie purging routines to ensure consecutive students do not inherit previous learners' session states. We provide detailed administrative documentation assisting school IT specialists in configuring automated browser profile resets across managed Chromebook and iPad fleets. Our functional tracking architecture prioritizes learner convenience while upholding the highest standards of classroom data segregation.

4. Managing Cookie Preferences, Browser Controls, and Opt-Outs

NumeracyBoost places complete control over digital cookie preferences directly into the hands of our users, parents, and institutional administrators. Upon your initial visit to our public domain, a prominent, accessible cookie preference banner provides immediate choices to accept all cookies, configure granular preferences, or reject all non-essential tracking scripts. You can revisit and modify your stored tracking consent preferences at any time by clicking the persistent 'Cookie Preferences' link situated in the footer of every web page. When you adjust your preferences to decline analytical tracking, our systems immediately transmit signal instructions that deactivate third-party analytics scripts and purge associated tracking tokens from your client browser. Our preference management mechanism operates instantaneously, requiring no cumbersome email correspondence or verification delays. We respect your sovereign privacy choices without imposing artificial navigational penalties or limiting access to open curriculum previews.

In addition to our on-site consent controls, all modern web browsers provide built-in administrative mechanisms allowing users to inspect, block, or delete cookies according to individual security preferences. You can configure your browser settings to universally block third-party cookies, alert you whenever a cookie is transmitted, or automatically purge all temporary cookies whenever you close your browser window. Detailed instructions for managing cookie controls are readily available within the official support documentation of major browsers, including Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Apple Safari, and Microsoft Edge. Please note that if you choose to universally block all cookies at the browser level, including strictly essential security tokens, you will be unable to log into authenticated student and teacher dashboards. Furthermore, NumeracyBoost actively recognizes and complies with automated browser-level privacy signals such as the Global Privacy Control (GPC) and Do Not Track (DNT) header protocols.

5. Security Audits, Policy Revisions, and Inquiries Directory

Digital tracking technologies and educational privacy regulations evolve continually, requiring rigorous ongoing institutional oversight and technological modernization. NumeracyBoost conducts quarterly cryptographic security audits and automated cookie scans across all public and authenticated web endpoints to ensure zero unauthorized script injection or tracking drift. If our software engineering team develops new interactive learning features that require novel cookie classifications or expanded telemetry storage, this policy is updated promptly to reflect those modifications. When material revisions affecting user privacy or data handling covenants are introduced, prominent electronic notices will be displayed across our learning portal for thirty calendar days prior to enforcement. Previous historical editions of our Cookie Policy are cataloged within our public compliance archives, allowing educators and researchers to review the evolution of our digital tracking governance over time.

We welcome constructive dialogue with educators, academic researchers, and digital privacy advocates regarding our cookie deployment protocols and pupil protection standards. If you have questions regarding the technical mechanics of our cookies, require assistance configuring institutional firewall whitelists, or wish to report a suspected tracking anomaly, please contact our Digital Governance Division. Electronic correspondence submitted to privacy@numeracyboost.com receives immediate tracking logging and a comprehensive technical response within two business days. Institutional school district coordinators may also request formal technical architecture consultations with our lead cybersecurity engineers to verify compliance with local school board privacy mandates. NumeracyBoost remains deeply dedicated to demonstrating that world-class quantitative education can be delivered with absolute digital transparency, ethical data stewardship, and uncompromised learner security.
