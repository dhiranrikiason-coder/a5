Student Data Governance

Privacy Policy & Pupil Record Protection

Comprehensive documentation of our pupil data privacy protocols, cryptographic storage standards, and institutional compliance mechanisms across digital learning environments.

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Last Revised: February 2026 • Regulatory Review Status: Fully Certified

1. Scope and Institutional Commitment to Pupil Privacy

NumeracyBoost is deeply committed to maintaining the highest global standards of privacy, confidentiality, and data integrity for all learners, educators, and institutional subscribers. This privacy policy applies to all interactions with our online educational modules, quantitative assessment suites, cognitive tracking tools, and parent communication dashboards. When students engage with our mathematical learning platforms, we collect only the minimal data strictly necessary to evaluate mathematical concept mastery and personalize instructional pacing. We do not monetize student personal records, sell analytical behavioral profiles, or license quantitative benchmark scores to third-party marketing entities under any circumstances. Every operational team member undergoes rigorous annualized background checks and compulsory training regarding pupil confidentiality protocols and digital security measures. By utilizing our academic platforms, parents, schools, and adult scholars acknowledge and consent to the principled collection and protective handling described herein.

Our privacy practices are intentionally structured to conform with international and domestic regulatory frameworks governing educational technology and children's digital well-being. These frameworks specifically include the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA), the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and applicable state student privacy statutes. Because our instructional curricula frequently engage minors under the age of thirteen, we enforce strict verified parental or institutional consent mechanisms prior to persistent profile establishment. School districts and independent academies contracting with NumeracyBoost maintain complete operational ownership over all student educational records created within their administrative enterprise domain. We serve strictly as a designated educational service provider operating under documented school authorization and contractual safeguards. We continually audit our digital perimeter and computational pipelines to ensure zero unauthorized exposure of sensitive youth demographic data.

2. Categories of Information Collected and Evaluative Telemetry

When an individual account or classroom roster is registered within our numerical ecosystem, we collect foundational directory details such as educator names, institutional email addresses, student first names, and grade level designations. We deliberately refrain from capturing unnecessary personal identifiers, including social security numbers, biometric markers, home addresses, or financial information belonging to minors. As learners progress through problem sets, geometric simulations, and timed mental arithmetic challenges, our servers record technical learning telemetry including problem completion latencies, error frequencies, and conceptual retry patterns. This quantitative telemetry is utilized exclusively by our machine learning models to map cognitive knowledge frontiers and generate targeted remediation pathways. All diagnostic scores and longitudinal progress trajectories are securely linked to pseudonymized internal account tokens rather than publicly discoverable student identities. Educators retain real-time transparency over all captured performance metrics through authenticated institutional reporting interfaces.

In addition to student learning interactions, our web servers automatically log standard network telemetry necessary for maintaining server availability, load balancing, and cybersecurity incident response. This standard technical metadata encompasses internet protocol (IP) addresses, device hardware architectures, web browser classifications, operating system versions, and timestamped page request headers. We aggregate this computational telemetry to diagnose rendering inconsistencies across diverse school computing labs, optimize server response latencies, and mitigate distributed denial-of-service attempts. Network telemetry is automatically purged from active operational storage after ninety days of rolling retention, transitioning to encrypted offline backups subject to scheduled destruction. We never correlate raw network connectivity logs with individual student academic files for behavioral advertising or commercial consumer profiling purposes. Third-party analytical scripts embedded on public marketing pages are strictly quarantined away from authenticated student classroom environments.

3. Cryptographic Storage Standards, Encryption, and Data Retention

All student records, mathematical diagnostic assessments, and educator communications are safeguarded utilizing multi-layered cryptographic defenses aligned with modern cybersecurity standards. Data in transit between client web browsers and our cloud computing infrastructure is universally encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3) protocols with forward secrecy. At rest, all persistent database records, analytical data lakes, and offsite system backups are protected via Advanced Encryption Standard (AES-256) bit cryptographic ciphers. Cryptographic keys are managed within dedicated hardware security modules with strict role-based access policies and comprehensive multi-factor authentication requirements. Physical server facilities maintaining our cloud infrastructure feature continuous biometric access controls, redundant power architectures, and rigorous SOC-2 Type II independent security certifications. In the unlikely event of an unauthorized security anomaly, we maintain established incident response procedures providing prompt notification to impacted school administrators and regulatory authorities.

Our data retention protocols are designed around pedagogical necessity, regulatory retention horizons, and explicit institutional instruction. Active student accounts and cumulative skill mastery profiles are retained throughout the active academic subscription term to enable continuous longitudinal benchmarking. Upon formal contract conclusion or written request from an authorized school district official, all associated pupil records are permanently expunged or anonymized within forty-five calendar days. Inactive guest assessment sessions completed without persistent account registration are automatically purged from volatile server memory within twenty-four hours of diagnostic report generation. We perform cryptographic data destruction routines that ensure deleted records cannot be recovered through forensic data reconstruction utilities. Parents and legal guardians possess the unambiguous right to inspect, correct, or demand the total deletion of their child's accumulated mathematical profile at any time.

4. Third-Party Service Providers and Interoperability Partners

To deliver high-availability cloud computing, automated diagnostic evaluation, and dependable transactional communications, NumeracyBoost collaborates with carefully vetted technical infrastructure vendors. These specialized service partners include tier-one cloud hosting facilities, managed database operators, email notification relays, and academic research statistical computing clusters. Prior to onboarding any external technical vendor, our legal and information security teams execute comprehensive vendor security reviews and legally binding Data Processing Agreements. These agreements legally obligate all subcontractors to maintain technical security safeguards equal to or exceeding our own institutional standards, while strictly prohibiting independent secondary use of pupil records. Vendor access to data streams is tightly restricted via principle-of-least-privilege permissions, ensuring subcontractors only access the specific database segments required for system operations. We publish and maintain a current registry of authorized technical sub-processors accessible to institutional administrators upon request.

Our platform also supports standardized educational software interoperability protocols, allowing seamless integration with institutional Learning Management Systems (LMS) such as Canvas, Google Classroom, and Schoology. When a school district enables Single Sign-On (SSO) or automated roster synchronization via industry standards such as OneRoster or LTI, data transfer occurs strictly over encrypted channels. We ingest only the roster attributes authorized by the local school district's technology coordinator, never requesting administrative privileges beyond core roster mapping. District administrators maintain granular controls enabling them to revoke third-party API keys or terminate automated synchronization integrations instantly through their centralized dashboard. We never share student assessment scores or personal information with external social media networks, consumer search engines, or untrusted commercial ed-tech marketplaces. Any future modification to our third-party sharing architecture requires proactive advance notification and explicit institutional re-authorization.

5. Parental Rights, Account Access, and Contact Directory

Under applicable educational privacy legislation, parents, legal guardians, and adult students retain comprehensive rights regarding their personal educational data records. Parents have the documented right to review the specific mathematical telemetry and diagnostic skill evaluations recorded for their minor child throughout the instructional period. If a parent identifies factual inaccuracies or outdated biographical information within an active learner profile, they may submit a formal correction request directly through their family portal. Furthermore, parents may instruct NumeracyBoost to cease further collection or processing of their child's quantitative learning data, prompting immediate account suspension and systematic data de-identification. When student accounts are established through a public school district or accredited private academy, parents may coordinate their data requests through their school's authorized FERPA records officer. Our dedicated student privacy desk provides continuous assistance to ensure parent inquiries receive prompt, transparent, and legally compliant resolution.

We maintain an open and accountable dialogue with educators, parents, and computational researchers regarding all facets of our data governance framework. Any questions, formal records requests, or regulatory privacy concerns should be directed immediately to our Chief Privacy Officer and Academic Compliance Division. Inquiries submitted electronically through our designated privacy portal receive formal tracking numbers and guaranteed written responses within two business days. Regular institutional audits ensure that our internal compliance teams continually align our daily operational workflows with evolving international privacy jurisprudence and ethical artificial intelligence standards. We encourage school board leaders, academic researchers, and parent-teacher associations to examine our published security documentation and transparent operational covenants. NumeracyBoost remains steadfast in its core conviction that advanced mathematical education must flourish hand-in-hand with uncompromised human dignity and absolute pupil privacy.
